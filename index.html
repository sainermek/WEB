Minecraft PvP Championship

Official Competitive Season 2026

Welcome to the ultimate dueling arena! Participate in Sword, Axe, Crystal, and Pot PvP tournaments, claim your rank in the official tiers (HT1/LT1), and prove your dominance.

Official Disciplines (Kits)

  • Sword (1.9+) — Classic sword duels requiring precise spacing, timing, and aim.
  • Axe PvP — Heavy combat utilizing shields and axes, focusing on critical hits and shield disables.
  • Crystal PvP — Fast-paced end crystal and obsidian placement combat.
  • Pot PvP — Speed-focused 1.8.9 duels using instant health and regeneration potions.

Tournament Rules

  1. Any modifications providing unfair advantages (AutoClicker, Reach, KillAura) are strictly prohibited.
  2. Players must agree to a Screen Share (SS) check upon request by match staff.
  3. Failure to show up within 10 minutes results in an automatic forfeiture.
  4. Maintain respectful conduct — toxicity and chat abuse lead to immediate disqualification.
